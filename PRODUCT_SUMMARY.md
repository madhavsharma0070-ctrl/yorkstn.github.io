# What Is Yorkstn? (Plain-English Summary)

## 1. What this product is

Yorkstn is a software platform that helps international consumer brands enter and grow in the Indian market. Think of it as a single online workspace that replaces a scattered mess of lawyers, spreadsheets, consultants, and guesswork with one connected system — covering the research, paperwork, partner-finding, and expansion planning a brand needs to actually launch in India.

It's a **SaaS product** (a web app you log into), with an **optional add-on**: if a brand doesn't want to do a task themselves, Yorkstn's own team can do it for them, tracked inside the same platform.

## 2. Who it's for

International consumer brands — the kind of company that sells clothing, kids' products, beauty and personal care items, home goods, or similar lifestyle products — that want to sell in India but don't have a local team or local expertise yet.

Inside a brand's team, the platform is built for the people actually doing the work: founders/owners, operations managers, compliance/legal staff, and analysts. It also has a separate section for the local partners (manufacturers, distributors, retail landlords) a brand works with, and for Yorkstn's own staff who help fulfill service requests.

## 3. What problems it solves

Launching a foreign brand in India today typically means juggling:
- Confusing, fast-changing regulations (company registration, import rules, tax registration, product certification, trademarks)
- No single, reliable source for "which city, which mall, which partner should I actually pick"
- No easy way to find and vet trustworthy local manufacturing/retail/distribution partners
- No connected view of "where am I in my India launch, and what's next"
- Needing to hire expensive one-off consultants for basic guidance

Yorkstn's bet is that no single existing tool does all of this together — most competitors solve one piece (compliance, or partner-sourcing, or market research) but not the whole journey. Yorkstn combines all of it into one workspace, with expert human help available on-demand for the trickiest steps, instead of forcing brands to use pure self-service software or pure consulting.

## 4. What the main modules/features are

Yorkstn has **four core modules**, plus one add-on service:

1. **AI Market Intelligence** — Research and analysis for a brand's category: market sizing, who the local competitors are, how to price products for India, demand forecasts, and which cities look most promising to launch in. Also gives a "Readiness Score" that tells a brand how prepared they actually are to launch.

2. **Compliance Operating System** — Step-by-step guidance and tracking for the legal/regulatory side of entering India: choosing the right company structure, import and customs rules, tax (GST) registration, product safety certification (BIS), and trademark protection. Includes a place to store and manage the documents involved.

3. **Partner Discovery Platform** — A searchable directory of vetted local partners (manufacturers, distributors, retail landlords), a way to request an introduction, and a self-service area where those partners can manage their own profile and get verified.

4. **Retail Expansion Intelligence** — Helps a brand decide *where* and *how* to physically expand: city and mall-level data, a scoring system for comparing potential store sites, a step-by-step expansion roadmap, financial projections, and a launch checklist.

**Plus — Managed Services:** At any point, a brand can request expert human help on a specific task (for example, "handle my company registration for me") directly from inside the platform. Yorkstn staff pick up the request and post updates the brand can see in real time, so nothing happens over email or in a black box.

## 5. What has already been built

**Everything above is fully built.** All four core modules and the Managed Services add-on are complete, working end-to-end, and have been tested — including who's allowed to see and do what (a brand's own team members have different permission levels, from full control down to view-only).

The system also includes the everyday things a real product needs behind the scenes: user accounts and logins, a company/team setup process, a record of who-did-what for accountability, and an automated check that runs every time code changes to make sure nothing is broken.

Right now, the product runs on realistic sample data and a "practice mode" for its AI features — meaning anyone can try the full product today without needing to connect it to paid outside services first.

## 6. What is still left to do for launch, if anything

Nothing that's missing is a missing *feature* — the product itself is done. What's left falls into two buckets, neither of which stops someone from trying the product today:

- **Small polish items:** for example, invitation emails currently work via a shareable link instead of an automatic email — functional, just not as slick as it could be.
- **Real-world setup for a public launch:** connecting the product to production-grade infrastructure instead of the local practice setup it currently runs on — a real database server, file storage, a live AI service, and error monitoring. None of this is difficult or in question, it just hasn't been switched on yet because it requires real accounts/credentials from the Yorkstn team. There's also a recommended step of validating some of the regulatory research with a lawyer/official source before it's shown to a real paying customer, and running early customer conversations to confirm the product matches what brands actually want.

## 7. The one-minute version

Yorkstn is a finished, working software product that helps foreign consumer brands launch in India — combining market research, legal/compliance guidance, partner-finding, and expansion planning into one platform, with optional expert help on demand. All of it is built and demoable right now. What remains is turning on production infrastructure and doing standard pre-launch validation — not building anything new.
